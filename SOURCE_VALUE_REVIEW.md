## B: Conditional Economics and Time

P3 verifies an official Z.ai campaign for the selected model, with a named timezone,
an overnight recurring window and paid-plan, model, channel and client-version
conditions. It also announces an extension. Date labels are metadata, not exact
version/effective instants: end-date inclusivity and the final overnight window
are unspecified; no durable predecessor or extension acquisition timestamp was
captured. The campaign terminal boundary remains uncertain. It is unsafe to
convert that date into an invented exclusive UTC timestamp or claim exact expiry.

Current credits plans and legacy plan generations use distinct rules. Campaign
quota conditions, separate off-peak rules and API token prices cannot simply be
multiplied together. A public exhaustion/reset condition does not disclose a
private account balance or guarantee participation for any user.

U4 explicitly acknowledges that its date-only Z.ai pricing transition approximates
a stated sub-day boundary. It also labels Coding Plan usage at API rates; that
valuation is not subscription marginal billing. These are concrete inspected
limitations, not a global claim that upstreams cannot handle time. P3's campaign
and U4's historical API-price promotion are separate subjects.

The R2 synthetic tests demonstrate supplied boundary behavior and retrieval-only
silence, not the real campaign's exact expiry under failed refresh. No real-source
fixture, fetcher, freshness policy or time-driven campaign simulation is added.
